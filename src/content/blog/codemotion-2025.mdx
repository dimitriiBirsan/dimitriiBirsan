I went to Codemotion Milan 2025 for the talks. A lot of what I remember now happened between them: meeting other developers and talking about the work we were each trying to figure out.

## Beyond the programme

I like that part of a conference. You get past the finished presentation and into the questions that are still open: what’s been difficult, what someone tried, and what they might do differently next time.

I wrote a longer reflection on those conversations and the community around the event.
