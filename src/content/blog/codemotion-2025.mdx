We often measure the success of a tech conference by the lines of code we see or the new frameworks we discover. But Codemotion Milan 2025 offered something different—a reminder that the true core of technology is the people who build it.

Beyond the keynotes and the technical deep-dives, this year's event was a journey of connection. It was about the conversations sparked in the hallways, the shared curiosity between strangers, and the realization that our community is built on more than just syntax.

In my latest article, **"More Than Code,"** I explore the human side of the conference and why, sometimes, the most valuable takeaways aren't found on the slides.
